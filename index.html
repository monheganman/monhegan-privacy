Privacy Policy — Monhegan Trails

Effective 28 August 2026

Monhegan Trails collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. The app makes no network connections of any kind, and nothing you do in it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location to show where you are on the Monhegan Island trail map, and your compass heading to orient the map. Location is also used to remember the single most recent place where your phone had cellular service, so that you can find your way back to it in an emergency.

To keep that point current while your phone is locked or in a pocket, location continues updating in the background while the app is running.

Your location is used entirely on your device. It is:

  • never transmitted — the app has no server and makes no network requests;
  • never written to storage — it is held in memory only;
  • discarded when you close the app — including the last-known-signal point, which does not survive a relaunch.

No history or track of your movements is kept at any time. You can turn location off for this app at any time in iOS Settings; the map still works, but it cannot show where you are.

The map

The trail map is stored inside the app and works entirely offline. Viewing it, zooming, and panning involve no network activity and are not recorded.

Emergency features

The app includes an emergency screen that can place a call to 911 or open a text message to 911 with your coordinates already filled in.

These are the only circumstances in which information about you leaves your device, and they happen only when you choose them:

  • Calling 911 hands off to your phone's normal dialler. The call is handled by your mobile carrier and the emergency network exactly as any 911 call would be.
  • Texting 911 opens your phone's standard message composer with the recipient and a message containing your coordinates prepared for you. The app cannot send it. iOS requires you to read the message and tap Send yourself, and you can edit or cancel it first.

Anything you send this way goes to emergency services through your carrier. It is not routed through the developer, and no copy is kept by the app.

Data collected by the developer

None. There is no server, no database, no logging, and no way for the developer to learn that you have used the app or what you did in it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code, no software development kits, no trackers, and no advertising. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

TestFlight beta versions

While the app is in beta testing, it is distributed through Apple's TestFlight service. Apple collects information about installations, crashes, and any feedback a tester chooses to submit, and provides it to the developer. That collection is Apple's, not the app's, and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Crash reports and tester feedback may include your device model, iOS version, and the app build you are running.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins collecting or transmitting information, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
